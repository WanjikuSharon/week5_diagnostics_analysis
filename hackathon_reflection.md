# Hackathon #1 Reflection

Hackathon #1 was a valuable learning experience that strengthened both my technical and teamwork skills. The biggest technical hurdle our team faced was performing a complete operational diagnostics analysis while ensuring our visualizations clearly communicated the findings. We initially struggled to organize the data analysis workflow and identify the most appropriate charts for anomaly detection, root cause analysis, and operational reporting.

We resolved this challenge by dividing the work among team members, reviewing the dataset together, and validating each other's analyses before combining our results. Using Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn allowed us to clean the data, identify anomalies, perform correlation analysis, and create meaningful visualizations that supported our conclusions.

The hackathon also highlighted the importance of effective communication. Regular discussions helped us stay aligned, share ideas, and identify errors early. Receiving feedback from teammates improved the quality of our final presentation and strengthened our confidence in explaining the results.

For the next hackathon, I would focus on assigning responsibilities earlier, scheduling more frequent progress check-ins, and integrating our work sooner rather than waiting until the final stages. This would reduce last-minute revisions and improve collaboration, allowing the team to spend more time refining insights and preparing a stronger final presentation.
