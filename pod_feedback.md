# Week 5 Pod Feedback Summary

## Pod Discussion Summary

During the pod presentation, I presented my operational diagnostics findings, including the anomaly detection, Pareto analysis, and correlation analysis. The team acted as Operations Directors and challenged my conclusions to ensure they were supported by evidence.

### Question 1
**"How do you know that maintenance is the main cause of reduced throughput?"**

**My Response**

I explained that the conclusion was supported by multiple analyses rather than a single chart. The box plot identified significant throughput anomalies, the Pareto analysis showed that missed maintenance accounted for most operational incidents, and the correlation heatmap showed a strong negative correlation (-0.85) between maintenance events and throughput.

**Improvement Made**

I clarified that correlation does not automatically imply causation. Instead, I explained that multiple analytical techniques consistently pointed toward maintenance as the strongest operational driver.

---

### Question 2
**"Could temperature be causing the production issues instead?"**

**My Response**

I referred to the correlation heatmap, which showed only a weak positive correlation (0.25) between temperature and throughput. This suggested that temperature had much less influence than maintenance activities.

**Improvement Made**

I strengthened my explanation by comparing the strength of the correlations instead of simply stating that temperature was not important.

---

### Question 3
**"Why should management focus on missed maintenance first?"**

**My Response**

I demonstrated that the Pareto analysis showed a small number of incident categories accounted for most operational disruptions. Addressing missed maintenance would therefore provide the greatest operational improvement with available resources.

**Improvement Made**

I linked the recommendation directly to the Pareto Principle (80/20 rule), making the business justification clearer.

---

## Overall Feedback Received

- Explain findings using business language instead of technical terms.
- Clearly distinguish between correlation and causation.
- Support every recommendation with evidence from the analysis.
- Summarize the key findings before presenting recommendations.

## How I Improved My Presentation

Based on the feedback, I:

- Improved the flow from analysis to business decisions.
